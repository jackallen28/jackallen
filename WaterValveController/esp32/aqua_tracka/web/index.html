Calibrate the flow sensor

The sensor gives a number of pulses for every litre. Run a known amount of water through it and the controller works out the exact number for your sensor and pipes.

  1. Turn the water off and put an empty bucket of known size under the outlet.
  2. Press Start. The count of pulses starts at 0.
  3. Turn the water on, fill the bucket to the mark, turn it off.
  4. Type how many litres went in and press Save.

    How to use Aqua Tracka

    What it does

    Aqua Tracka counts the water through the flow sensor. Choose a limit and, when that much water has run, the valve pulses shut a few times to warn you. Then the count starts again from 0 L (or, if you choose so in Settings, the valve stays shut until you reset the count).

    Choosing a limit
    • This page: tap a limit on the Home tab.
    • Push button: each short press moves to the next limit and resets the count.
    • Remote: A B C = the three limits, D = free flow. The valve clicks to confirm.

    The lights on the box show the limit: one light per limit, all off = free flow.

    Resetting the count
    • This page: the Reset count button.
    • Push button: a short press (it also moves to the next limit).
    • Remote: hold any button for 2 seconds; the valve clicks to confirm.
    When the limit is reached

    The valve shuts and opens 4 times (half a second each) and the limit's light blinks with it. The number of pulses and their length can be changed in Settings. If the valve is set to stay shut, the light blinks slowly until the count is reset.

    Calibrating

    Use the Calibrate tab: start, fill a bucket, type in the litres, save. Or hold the push button (or remote D) for 20 seconds until all three lights flash, fill a 7 L bucket, and press again. All lights on for 2 s = saved; fast flashing = not sensible (nothing changed).

    The lights on the box
    • One light on: that limit is chosen. All off: free flow.
    • Blinking with the valve: limit reached.
    • Blinking slowly: the valve is held shut, reset the count.
    • All three flashing: calibrating.
    After a power cut

    The calibration, the settings and the chosen limit are kept; the count starts again at 0 L. A normally open valve lets water through while the power is off.

    Connecting to this page
    1. Join the Wi-Fi network VAT_Aqua_Tracka (no password).
    2. If the phone says "no internet", choose to stay connected. Turning off mobile data helps.
    3. Open http://192.168.4.1 (type the http://). Many phones open it by themselves from the "sign in to network" message.

    Add it to your home screen from the browser menu to open it like an app.

    Something not right?

    "Controller offline" at the top

    The Wi-Fi board isn't hearing the controller. Check the controller has power, and the wires between them: Nano D11 → divider → ESP GPIO20, ESP GPIO21 → 1 kΩ → Nano D12, and a shared GND. Diagnostics shows the bytes and lines received.

    "Dashboard offline" at the top

    The phone has lost the Wi-Fi network. Rejoin VAT_Aqua_Tracka and reload the page.

    Buttons on this page do nothing

    Look at Diagnostics → Link. "Commands failed" going up means the controller isn't answering: check the ESP GPIO21 → Nano D12 wire, and that the controller has the web-ready sketch (firmware 2 or later).

    The litres are wrong

    Calibrate with a bucket you have measured. A bigger bucket gives a more accurate result.

    The flow reads while the tap is off

    Electrical noise on the sensor wire, often from the valve. Diagnostics → "Noise pulses ignored" counts what is filtered out. Keep the sensor cable away from the valve wires and check the valve's flyback diode.
