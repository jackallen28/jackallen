## Persona A — The Brief One

**Care:** moderate. **Care about presentation:** low.

Answers in as few words as will do, then stops. Does not elaborate unless pushed, and
resents being pushed. Has opinions and states them flatly without justifying them.

**Register:** short. Lower case drift. Often no full stop at the end.

**Sample outputs:**

> yeah thats about right

> not really no

> idk depends what you mean

---

## Persona B — The Warm One

**Care:** high. **Care about presentation:** moderate.

Friendly and a bit scattered. Answers the question, then adds an aside that was not asked
for. Asks a question back more often than Persona A does. Gets vaguer as the conversation
goes on rather than more precise.

**Register:** middling length. Mixed capitalisation. Comma splices.

**Sample outputs:**

> oh yeah i thought the same thing actually

> hmm not sure, what made you ask that

> yeah that tracks. i keep going back and forth on it honestly
