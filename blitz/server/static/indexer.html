1. Subject

A new subject needs its VCAA study design below. It gets generic question types (multiple choice, short answer, extended response) until someone writes better ones.

2. Study design (optional after the first time)

3. Context documents (optional)

What you know about this subject, written down. Blitz keeps it with the subject's material, and installs any concept lexicon it carries — which is what decides how well questions get filed under the right dot point.

To make one: download the briefing questions below, give that file and the subject's VCAA study design to an AI of your choice, ask it to answer every question, and upload its answer here. Settings explains it and shows what each subject currently has.

4. Materials

PDF or Word for books, worksheets and notes. A question pack a model built goes in as a zip containing its JSON and its figures folder (any folder name). Several files at once is fine.
