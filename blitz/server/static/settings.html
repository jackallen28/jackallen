Subjects

Each subject has a folder holding its dot points, its indexing notes and anything you add. Adding a new subject is done by uploading its study design on the Index materials page.

Teaching Blitz about a subject

Blitz files a question under a dot point by matching the words the question uses against the words a question about that dot point tends to use. The study design does not supply those words, so a subject without a concept lexicon falls back on word overlap, which is much weaker. Three steps to fix that for any subject:

  1. Download its briefing questions above.
  2. Give that file and the subject's VCAA study design to an AI of your choice, and ask it to answer every question. It walks through how the study design breaks up, which dot points are confusable, and what vocabulary each one actually attracts.
  3. Save the answer and upload it back as a context document. Blitz keeps the notes with the subject and installs the lexicon at the end of it, after checking every dot point id against the study design.

The study design is a public VCAA document. Do not paste copyrighted textbook content into an AI as part of this.

Backup

One zip with the index, every figure, the study designs, the student record and every sheet. Take it with you when the app is updated or moved; restore it with blitz restore <zip>, or on a new machine's first-run screen.
